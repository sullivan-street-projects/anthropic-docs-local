# Claude Sonnet 5

**Release Date:** June 30, 2026

**API Model ID:** `claude-sonnet-5`

## Positioning

Claude Sonnet 5 is built to be "the most agentic Sonnet model yet," with performance approaching Opus 4.8 at substantially lower cost. It is a substantial improvement over its predecessor, Sonnet 4.6, on core agentic dimensions: reasoning, tool use, coding, and knowledge work.

## Pricing

- Input: $2 per million tokens (permanent pricing as of August 2026; originally introduced as introductory pricing, the previously scheduled increase to $3/$15 will not occur)
- Output: $10 per million tokens

## Availability

Available across all plans:

- Default model for **Free** and **Pro** users
- Available to **Max**, **Team**, and **Enterprise** users
- Available in **Claude Code** and on the **Claude Platform / API** (`claude-sonnet-5`)

## Key Capabilities

- Substantial improvement over Sonnet 4.6 on reasoning, tool use, coding, and knowledge work
- Makes plans and uses tools like browsers and terminals autonomously
- Finishes complex tasks where previous Sonnet models would stop short
- Checks its own output without being explicitly asked
- Strong agentic performance at a lower price point than Opus-tier models
- Sustained focus on multi-step tasks
- Supports variable effort levels for cost-performance optimization

## Safety Improvements

Sonnet 5 shows an overall lower rate of undesirable behaviors than Sonnet 4.6 and is generally safer to use in agentic contexts:

- Improved refusal of malicious requests
- Better resistance to prompt injection attacks
- Reduced hallucination and sycophancy rates
- Substantially weaker cybersecurity capabilities than Opus models
- Never successfully developed full exploits in Firefox vulnerability testing
- Cyber safeguards enabled by default across deployment platforms

## Technical Details

- Updated tokenizer affects token mapping (approximately 1.0-1.35x variance depending on content)
- Benchmarks show performance improvements approaching Opus 4.8 across reasoning, tool use, coding, and knowledge-work suites

## Real-World Validation

Multiple early-access partners highlighted that Sonnet 5 "finishes complex tasks where previous Sonnet models would stop short" and demonstrates improved follow-through on multi-step workflows without explicit prompting.
