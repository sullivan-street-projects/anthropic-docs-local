# Mariano-Florentino (Tino) Cuéllar to join Anthropic as Chief Global Affairs Officer

**Publication Date:** August 4, 2026

---

Mariano-Florentino (Tino) Cuéllar will become Anthropic's inaugural Chief Global Affairs Officer, overseeing the company's policy work, international strategic partnerships, and interactions with governments globally.

Cuéllar brings extensive experience across legal, technological, security, and institutional domains. He recently concluded his tenure as president of the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace, a prestigious independent policy research organization with scholars distributed across 20 nations. Previously, he served as a California Supreme Court Justice, where his rulings encompassed technology and privacy matters, international treaties, and constitutional separation of powers questions.

His background also includes directing Stanford's Freeman Spogli Institute for International Studies, co-directing the university's Center for International Security and Cooperation, and heading the Stanford Cyber Initiative. He has contributed to the President's Intelligence Advisory Board and the State Department's Foreign Affairs Policy Board, working across three presidential administrations.

Recently, Cuéllar co-chaired a bipartisan nuclear proliferation task force and led California's Frontier AI Working Group. He currently holds a professorship at Stanford Law School and serves as a senior fellow at Stanford's Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence.

According to Cuéllar, "there is no more consequential place to be shaping that work right now than Anthropic." Daniela Amodei, expressing enthusiasm about the hire, noted that Cuéllar has consistently demonstrated "sound judgment and civic-mindedness" throughout his career.
