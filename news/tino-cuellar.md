# Mariano-Florentino Cuéllar Joins Anthropic as Chief Global Affairs Officer

**Date:** August 4, 2026

## Summary

Anthropic announced that Mariano-Florentino (Tino) Cuéllar will serve as the company's inaugural Chief Global Affairs Officer, overseeing policy matters, international strategy, and government relations globally.

## Key Background

Cuéllar brings extensive experience across multiple sectors. He recently departed from his role as President of the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace. His previous positions include serving as a California Supreme Court Justice, directing Stanford's Freeman Spogli Institute for International Studies, and holding positions within three presidential administrations. Currently, he teaches at Stanford Law School and serves as a Senior Fellow at its Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence.

## Relevant Expertise

His qualifications encompass work on nuclear proliferation, California's Frontier AI Working Group, and the National Academy of Sciences' Committee on Responsible Computing Research. He previously served as trustee of Anthropic's Long-Term Benefit Trust, a position he has now relinquished.

## Company Statement

Daniela Amodei noted that Cuéllar's "sound judgment and civic-mindedness" across government, law, and academia make him well-suited to "partner with governments, civil society, and community groups" regarding AI's risks and opportunities.

## Context

The hiring reflects Anthropic's emphasis on engaging policymakers during what leadership views as a critical moment for AI governance and development.
