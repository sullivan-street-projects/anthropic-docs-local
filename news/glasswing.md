# Project Glasswing: Securing Critical Software for the AI Era

**Date:** April 7, 2026

---

## Introduction

Anthropic announced Project Glasswing, a collaborative initiative involving twelve major technology companies and organizations including Amazon Web Services, Apple, Broadcom, Cisco, CrowdStrike, Google, JPMorganChase, the Linux Foundation, Microsoft, NVIDIA, and Palo Alto Networks.

The initiative centers on Claude Mythos Preview, an unreleased frontier AI model that represents a significant leap in cybersecurity capabilities. According to the announcement, this model "can surpass all but the most skilled humans" at identifying and exploiting software vulnerabilities.

Key findings include:
- Mythos Preview has already discovered thousands of high-severity vulnerabilities
- Flaws were found in every major operating system and web browser
- Some vulnerabilities had remained undetected for decades despite extensive testing
- The model developed exploits with increasing sophistication

Anthropic is committing $100 million in model usage credits and $4 million in direct donations to open-source security organizations.

## Cybersecurity in the AI Age

Global cybercrime costs are estimated at approximately $500 billion annually. Critical infrastructure—banking systems, healthcare networks, energy grids, and government agencies—has suffered serious breaches from state-sponsored actors including China, Iran, North Korea, and Russia.

Historically, finding vulnerabilities required rare expertise held by elite security professionals. However, frontier AI models have fundamentally changed this landscape by dramatically reducing the cost and expertise barriers to vulnerability discovery and exploitation.

## Mythos Preview Capabilities

The model demonstrated superior performance across multiple evaluation benchmarks:

**Vulnerability Identification:**
- CyberGym benchmark: 83.1% (compared to Claude Opus 4.6's 66.6%)

**Coding and Reasoning Tasks:**
- SWE-bench Pro: 77.8% (vs. 53.4%)
- Terminal-Bench 2.0: 82.0% (vs. 65.4%)
- SWE-bench Multilingual: 87.3% (vs. 77.8%)

Notable vulnerabilities identified include:
- A 27-year-old flaw in OpenBSD
- A 16-year-old vulnerability in FFmpeg that automated testing had missed
- Multiple Linux kernel vulnerabilities that could enable privilege escalation

## Industry Partner Perspectives

Partners emphasized the urgency of the initiative. According to Cisco's Chief Security Officer, "AI capabilities have crossed a threshold that fundamentally changes the urgency required to protect critical infrastructure."

Microsoft's cybersecurity executive noted that "the window between a vulnerability being discovered and being exploited by an adversary has collapsed—what once took months now happens in minutes."

The Linux Foundation's CEO highlighted that "open source maintainers have historically been left to figure out security on their own," positioning this initiative as potentially transformative.

## Project Plans and Future Work

**Immediate Activities:**
- Partners will use Mythos Preview for vulnerability detection and system hardening
- Over 40 additional organizations have gained access to secure critical infrastructure
- Pricing: $25/$125 per million input/output tokens (after initial credit allocation)

**Medium-Term Goals:**
- Public reporting within 90 days on lessons learned and disclosed vulnerabilities
- Development of practical recommendations addressing:
  - Vulnerability disclosure processes
  - Software update mechanisms
  - Supply-chain security
  - Secure development lifecycle practices
  - Industry standards and patching automation

**Safeguards and Access:**
Anthropic does not plan general public release of Mythos Preview. Future Claude Opus models will incorporate new safeguards, with a Cyber Verification Program available for legitimate security professionals whose work may be affected.

The company is coordinating with U.S. government officials, recognizing that maintaining technological leadership in AI cybersecurity capabilities represents a national security priority for democratic nations.

## Project Naming

The initiative takes its name from the glasswing butterfly (*Greta oto*), whose transparent wings serve as a metaphor for both hidden vulnerabilities and the transparency Anthropic advocates in approach.
