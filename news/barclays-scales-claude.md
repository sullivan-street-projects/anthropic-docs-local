# Barclays scales Claude to upgrade operations and improve client experience

Anthropic, **October 1, 2026**. Barclays is expanding its strategic collaboration with Anthropic to bring enterprise-grade AI across its global operations — speeding software development, modernizing legacy systems, and improving operational efficiency.

- **Claude Code adoption:** expected to reach 50% of Barclays' developer population by end of 2026, and a majority of software engineers in 2027.
- **Colleague Knowledge Assistant** (powered by Claude, live since 2025): 16,000+ colleagues adopted; 1M+ searches; supports employees serving 20M+ UK retail customers.
- **Global Markets:** Claude classifies, enriches, and routes incoming client emails (~120,000/day).
- Governance, security controls, and human oversight applied across use cases.
