# Anthropic Raises $65B in Series H Funding

**Date:** May 28, 2026

## Overview

Anthropic secured $65 billion in Series H funding, reaching a $965 billion post-money valuation. The round was led by Altimeter Capital, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, and Sequoia Capital.

## Key Financial Metrics

- Run-rate revenue crossed $47 billion earlier in May 2026
- $15 billion from hyperscalers, including $5 billion from Amazon
- Additional commitments from strategic infrastructure partners: Micron, Samsung, and SK hynix

## Major Investors

**Co-leads:** Capital Group, Coatue, D1 Capital Partners, GIC, ICONIQ, XN

**Significant participants:** Blackstone, Brookfield, D.E. Shaw Ventures, DST Global, Fidelity, General Catalyst, Insight Partners, Jane Street, Lightspeed Venture Partners, and others.

## Compute Expansion

Anthropic signed major agreements for capacity expansion:

- Amazon: up to five gigawatts
- Google and Broadcom: five gigawatts of next-generation TPU capacity
- SpaceX: GPU access in Colossus 1 and Colossus 2

Claude is now available on all three major cloud platforms: AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure.

## Use of Funds

The funding will support safety and interpretability research, expanded compute infrastructure, and product scaling for tools like Claude Code and Cowork.

## Strategic Significance

Investors emphasized strong enterprise adoption and Claude's integration into core business operations globally.
