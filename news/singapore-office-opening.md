# Anthropic opens Singapore office and signs MOU with the Government of Singapore

Anthropic, **October 14, 2026**. A new Singapore office — Anthropic's fifth in Asia-Pacific — and an MOU with the Singapore Ministry of Digital Development and Information. Claude already supports government and business services there (GovTech Singapore, Grab, DBS). Dale Finlay (GM, ASEAN) said Anthropic is "committed to Singapore for the long term."

## The MOU

Signed at Singapore International Cyber Week by Permanent Secretary Kai Fong Chng and co-founder Ben Mann (witnessed by Minister Josephine Teo and Michael Sellitto, Head of APAC Policy). It covers cooperation on AI safety, security, and model evaluation; research on AI adoption and its effects; and responsible adoption across public and private sectors. Anthropic's Singapore work dates to 2023 (Generative AI Evaluation Sandbox with IMDA and the AI Verify Foundation; a red-teaming project in English, Mandarin, Malay, and Tamil).

Planned under the MOU: technical exchanges on model evaluation, testing standards, and incident response; strengthening AI skills; supporting SMEs in responsible adoption; building public-officer AI capability; and sharing research and data (including the Economic Index).

## Adoption examples

Singapore ranks 2nd of 121 countries in Claude.ai usage relative to population. 1,500+ GovTech engineers use Claude Code to modernize government systems; the Cyber Security Agency is exploring Claude for cyber defense. DBS uses Claude for software development; GIC is rolling it out across investment and corporate services. Regulated firms can run Claude with in-region inference on Amazon Bedrock. Grab's BriX sales assistant saves ~40,000 hours/quarter; Sea uses Claude for Shopee's customer-service chatbot; Supabase builds with Claude Code. A Builder Summit Singapore drew 300+ developers; a community meetup drew 700+ builders. The office is open and hiring.

## Significance

A long-term regional commitment, a formalized government partnership centered on AI safety and evaluation, and evidence of deep public-sector and regulated-finance adoption.
