# Partnering with Accenture on embedded evaluation

**Publication Date:** September 18, 2026

Anthropic has announced a partnership with Accenture to conduct independent evaluation of frontier AI systems. This initiative fulfills a commitment outlined in CEO Dario Amodei's essay "We Must Pace the Frontier" to embed evaluators within the company.

## Partnership Details

The collaboration will be managed through Faculty, Accenture's specialist AI business division. Their responsibilities will encompass "evaluating and red-teaming models, conducting alignment assessments, and testing model safeguards." Accenture's expertise in enterprise AI deployment across industries will inform their safety methodology.

Both organizations plan to invest at least $1 billion over five years to develop capacity in this evaluation area.

## How Embedded Evaluation Works

Unlike external evaluators, embedded evaluators will have employee-level access to observe model development throughout training, monitor deployment decisions, and communicate directly with staff. This proximity enables assessment of company operations, verification of safety commitments, and identification of organizational blind spots. They can also report incidents and provide public transparency regarding model benefits and risks.

The announcement emphasizes that "independent embedded evaluators do not reduce our accountability, but help to make it more verifiable."

## Funding and Standards

Currently, no established standards exist for evaluator access or reporting protocols. Anthropic funds Accenture's work directly while also collaborating with nonprofit evaluators like METR using alternative arrangements. The company advocates for long-term funding through pooled or government sources, as detailed in its Advanced AI Framework.

The partnership remains non-exclusive, with additional evaluators expected to be announced.
