# Improving Fable 5's Biology Safeguards

## Key Announcements

Anthropic has updated Claude Fable 5's biology safeguards to substantially reduce false positives. The company reports that this update reduced biology-related fallbacks by about 85%.

## Improved User Experience

Users will encounter significantly fewer "fallbacks"—instances where the system switches to the less capable Opus 5 model. The update aims to help with everyday health questions, educational biology topics, and clinical tasks for healthcare professionals.

## Remaining Restrictions

Fable 5 continues blocking dual-use requests involving virology, toxicology, and molecular design. The company states it isn't yet usable for professional biology research and drug development but remains committed to creating trusted access pathways for frontier biology capabilities.

## Rationale for Safeguards

Anthropic emphasizes that Fable 5's capabilities present genuine risks. The model can provide significant uplift to malicious actors developing biological weapons. The company notes the fundamental challenge: beneficial research often requires producing the same dangerous compounds as harmful applications.

## Technical Implementation

The safeguards use automated AI classifiers that detect potentially harmful biology queries and redirect them to Opus 5. Anthropic refined the classifier's guidelines and retrained it based on expert feedback to better distinguish between benign and dual-use content.

## Ongoing Work

The company acknowledges more refinement is needed and invites continued user feedback.
