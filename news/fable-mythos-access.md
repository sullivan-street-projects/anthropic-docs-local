# Statement on the US Government Directive to Suspend Access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5

**Date:** June 12, 2026

## Summary

Anthropic announced it must disable access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models following a US government export control directive citing national security concerns. The company received the order at 5:21 PM ET and determined it must apply the suspension to all users, including foreign nationals and employees, to ensure compliance. Other Anthropic models remain unaffected.

## Key Details

**The Government's Concern:**
The directive referenced awareness of a "jailbreaking" method for Fable 5. When Anthropic reviewed the demonstrated technique, they found it involved asking the model to review code and identify flaws -- a capability reportedly available in competing systems like OpenAI's GPT-5.5.

**Anthropic's Defense Strategy:**
The company emphasized it implemented extensive safeguards through thousands of hours of red-teaming with government, international, and private partners. The statement noted that "perfect jailbreak resistance is not currently possible for any model provider" and adopted layered defenses including narrow jailbreak limitations and comprehensive monitoring.

**Company's Position:**
Anthropic disagreed with the suspension, arguing that applying this standard industry-wide would "essentially halt all new model deployments for all frontier model providers." The company stated it is complying while working to restore access.
