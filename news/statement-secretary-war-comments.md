# Statement on the comments from Secretary of War Pete Hegseth

**Publication Date:** February 27, 2026

Anthropic released a statement responding to Secretary of War Pete Hegseth's announcement that the Department of War would designate the company as a "supply chain risk."

## The Dispute

Negotiations between Anthropic and the Department of War stalled over two exceptions the company requested regarding Claude's use: prohibition on mass domestic surveillance of Americans and restrictions on fully autonomous weapons deployment.

## Anthropic's Position

The company states it supports "all lawful uses of AI for national security" except these two areas. They argue current AI models lack sufficient reliability for autonomous weapons and that mass surveillance violates fundamental rights.

## Legal Challenge

Anthropic plans to contest the designation in court, asserting the Secretary lacks statutory authority to restrict contractor use of Claude beyond Department of War contracts specifically.

## Customer Impact

The company clarifies the designation would only affect Department of War contract work. Individual customers and commercial contracts remain unaffected.

## Historical Context

Anthropic emphasizes this marks an "unprecedented" application of supply chain risk designation to an American company, historically reserved for U.S. adversaries.

The statement expresses deep concern about setting precedents for government negotiations with American technology companies.
