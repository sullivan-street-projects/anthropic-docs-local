# Expanding Project Glasswing

**Date:** Jun 2, 2026

---

Project Glasswing represents Anthropic's collaborative initiative to enhance software security globally. The program previously granted roughly 50 initial partners access to Claude Mythos Preview in early April, enabling them to scan codebases for vulnerabilities. These partners have identified "more than 10,000 high- or critical-severity security flaws."

## Expansion Details

The initiative now extends to approximately 150 additional organizations across more than 15 countries. These new participants represent sectors previously underrepresented, including power, water, healthcare, communications, and hardware infrastructure. Many are vendors whose codebases support numerous organizations worldwide, including government entities.

Each organization shares a critical distinction: "a successful attack on their codebase could be catastrophic." Anthropic estimates that major attacks against most partners could impact over 100 million people, with significant implications for global and national security.

## Strategic Purpose

The program aims to achieve two primary objectives. First, it helps the software industry adapt by safely providing access to advanced models and tools. Second, it shifts support from vulnerability detection toward disclosure, remediation, and deployment of patched software.

## Current Initiatives

Anthropic recently launched Claude Security, utilizing frontier models like Claude Opus 4.8 to identify vulnerabilities and suggest solutions. The company also released specialized tools to trusted security teams to accelerate vulnerability detection.

## Future Direction

The organization plans further expansion, prioritizing essential infrastructure providers and critical open-source software maintainers. Anthropic acknowledges developing robust safeguards remains essential before broader access becomes possible.
