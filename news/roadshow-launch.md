# Anthropic launches roadshow for proposed initial public offering

- **Published:** November 10, 2026
- **Category:** Announcements
- **Topic:** Funding

## Announcement

Anthropic, PBC has launched the roadshow for its proposed initial public offering (IPO) of Class A common stock.

### Offering terms

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Total shares offered | 825,000,000 Class A shares |
| Offered by Anthropic | 801,351,354 shares |
| Offered by selling stockholders | 23,648,646 shares (Anthropic receives no proceeds from these) |
| Underwriters' option | 30-day option to buy up to 123,750,000 additional Class A shares |
| Expected price range | $70.00 to $78.00 per share |
| Exchange / ticker | Nasdaq Global Select Market (application filed), symbol "ANT" |
| Lead underwriters | Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC and Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC |

### Prospectus

Copies of the preliminary prospectus are available from Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC and Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC.

### Legal notice

- The offering will be made only by means of a prospectus.
- A registration statement has been filed with the SEC but is not yet effective. The securities may not be sold, and offers to buy may not be accepted, before it becomes effective.
- The announcement is not an offer to sell or a solicitation of an offer to buy.
