# Anthropic Expands Partnership with Google and Broadcom for Next-Generation Compute

**Date:** April 6, 2026

## Key Announcement

Anthropic has signed a new agreement with Google and Broadcom to secure multiple gigawatts of next-generation TPU capacity expected to come online starting in 2027. This expansion will support Claude's frontier models and meet growing customer demand.

## Major Metrics

- **Run-rate revenue:** Surpassed $30 billion (up from ~$9 billion at end of 2025)
- **High-value customers:** Over 1,000 business customers now spending $1 million+ annually on an annualized basis, doubling in less than two months
- **Investment commitment:** Continuing the $50 billion investment in American AI infrastructure announced in November 2025

## Strategic Details

The partnership builds on Anthropic's existing Google Cloud relationship and expands TPU capacity. The vast majority of new compute will be located in the United States.

Krishna Rao, CFO, stated: "This groundbreaking partnership...is a continuation of our disciplined approach to scaling infrastructure."

## Infrastructure Diversification

Anthropic trains Claude on multiple hardware platforms—AWS Trainium, Google TPUs, and NVIDIA GPUs—enabling better performance and resilience. Amazon remains the primary cloud provider, and Claude is available on all three major cloud platforms: AWS Bedrock, Google Cloud Vertex AI, and Microsoft Azure Foundry.
