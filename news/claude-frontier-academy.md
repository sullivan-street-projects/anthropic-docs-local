# Claude Frontier Academy

Anthropic, **October 2, 2026**. A **$100 million** program to train 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers (FDEs) by the end of 2027, targeting the shortage of people who can put AI into production inside real businesses.

- **Launch partners:** Accenture, Bain, Capgemini, Commonwealth Bank of Australia, Deloitte, McKinsey, Morgan Stanley, Novo Nordisk, and others.
- **Model:** the flagship FDE Residency follows a "medical model" — learn from practitioners, practice on realistic cases, be assessed before working independently.
- **Structure:** (1) multi-day in-person session ending in a graded practical (Claude Resident Engineer badge); (2) 12-week residency leading a real Claude use case with Anthropic support; (3) final assessment (Claude Frontier Deployed Engineer badge, first expected early 2027).
- **Eligibility:** hands-on software engineers with strong fundamentals and LLM-building experience; organizations nominate participants.
- **Locations:** San Francisco, New York, London. Builds on the Claude Partner Network (175,000+ certifications across 46,000 firms).
