# Funding better evaluations of AI's impact on wellbeing

## Overview

Anthropic announced a $5 million grant program supporting independent research on how AI affects user wellbeing. The initiative provides funding, model access, and technical assistance to researchers developing open-source evaluation tools for measuring AI's impact on users.

## Key Challenges in Wellbeing Evaluation

The organization identifies wellbeing assessment as uniquely complex compared to other model evaluations. Unlike accuracy checks on single responses, wellbeing requires contextual understanding across conversations. The article illustrates this with an example: fitness advice appropriate for general audiences could harm someone with a history of disordered eating.

Anthropic notes that risks often emerge gradually through extended interactions, requiring evaluators to recognize when conversations escalate or context shifts substantially.

## Evaluation Standards

The program seeks evaluations that:

- State clearly what they are measuring (what counts as a pass or fail, and why it matters)
- Incorporate clinical and subject-matter experts in design and validation
- Assess both overcompliance risks and harm risks
- Represent realistic multi-turn conversation scenarios
- Validate graders against actual subject-matter experts

## Application Information

Applicants can access a detailed guidance document and application form. The deadline for initial applications is September 21, with full proposal notifications expected by October 5.
