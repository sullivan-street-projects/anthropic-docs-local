# Open call for guest investigators in the life sciences

Anthropic, **October 22, 2026**. Anthropic is inviting life sciences researchers to apply for **special projects** in which they work directly with Anthropic's researchers on Claude-supported investigations.

## Who can apply

Eligible applicants are **principal investigators and their research groups** at academic institutions, nonprofits, or government labs. Target areas include causal human biology, structural and computational molecular biology, medicinal chemistry, drug discovery, and clinical science.

## How it works

Selected projects run for **8 to 12 weeks**, during which Anthropic runs its models and harnesses **at no cost** to the investigators, who receive analyses, artifacts, and tools for their work. Outputs are to be **published**, with accompanying data and code **open-sourced**. Investigators keep the rights to their data and to any independent follow-on research.

Applicants must explain how they would benefit from the collaboration and show that preliminary outputs are achievable within the collaboration period, though some projects may be extended. Applications should only include publicly available information — Anthropic is not requesting applicants' data. The application window is about **14 days**, with selected participants notified roughly 14 days after it closes.

## Context

The program is part of broader efforts, alongside the **Life Sciences Verification Program** (direct model access) and the **AI for Science** initiative, which provides Claude credits to scientists.
