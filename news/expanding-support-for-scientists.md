# Expanding our support for scientists

## Overview

Anthropic is significantly expanding its support for the scientific research community through subscriptions, products, and funding programs.

## Claude Team Plan for Scientists

The company is launching a new subscription offering with 10,000 initial seats available globally. Standard seats are free, while premium seats with 5x usage limits cost $15 monthly. Eligible principal investigators and equivalent researchers at academic or nonprofit institutions can register through a verification form and add lab members to their plans. Anthropic intends to expand beyond the initial 10,000 seats in coming months.

## AI for Science Program Expansion

The existing AI for Science program, which provides free credits to researchers, is broadening its scope. Previously focused on biological sciences, it now supports researchers across multiple scientific disciplines, including those conducting ambitious, compute-heavy research like work on the Riemann zeta function and protein design. Researchers can apply for up to $50,000 in credits per project.

## Claude Science Product

The Claude Science workbench integrates commonly-used research tools, produces auditable outputs, and offers flexible computing resource access.

## Biology and Chemistry Research Access

Researchers in biology and chemistry remain limited to Opus-class models. Fable models continue blocking professional biology and drug development queries due to dual-use risks. Anthropic is partnering with the U.S. government to establish an access program for life sciences professionals using Mythos-class models, with initial participants already enrolled.
