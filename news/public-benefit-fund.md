# Investing in the public benefit

Anthropic, **October 18, 2026**. Anthropic announced the **Anthropic Public Benefit Fund** and is committing part of its equity to it.

## The commitment

- **5% of Anthropic's shares** go to the Fund at announcement, if stockholders approve.
- The board may add **2% for every 50% increase in company value** above a set baseline. As Anthropic grows, the share set aside for the public good grows with it.
- Tech companies often hand this kind of equity to executives as escalating compensation awards. Anthropic is sending it to the public benefit instead.

## Why a separate fund

Most of Anthropic's mission work happens inside the company, which is a public benefit corporation. That includes safety and interpretability research, applied biomedical research, and discounted Claude for nonprofits and scientific institutions. That work has both public-benefit and commercial value, so it stays in the company. The Fund gives more flexibility and scale. It could make large donations to traditional nonprofits or distribute money directly to specific populations.

Example: Anthropic has already committed **$200 million** to studying and addressing AI's effects on the labor market. The post says bolder approaches may be needed, and the Fund is meant to pay for them.

## Structure and governance

- The Fund is a **wholly-owned LLC**, not a 501(c)(3), so the forms of giving can be adjusted.
- Anthropic equity held by the LLC **will not be used for lobbying or political giving**, in the US or abroad.
- More detail on structure, governance and valuation milestones will come in the next few months.

## Builds on earlier commitments

- At founding, the co-founders pledged to donate **80% of their equity**.
- Under an **employee equity donation match**, Anthropic matches equity that employees pledge. About half of employees take part.
- With this announcement, about **20% of Anthropic shares** go to the public good, plus whatever the valuation milestones add.

Anthropic plans to publish a **public benefit report** on its progress against its mission.
