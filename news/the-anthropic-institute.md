# Introducing The Anthropic Institute

**March 11, 2026**

## Overview

Anthropic has launched The Anthropic Institute, described as "a new effort to confront the most significant challenges that powerful AI will pose to our societies." The initiative combines research teams to inform policymakers and the public about transformative AI risks and opportunities.

## Key Developments

The Institute consolidates three existing research programs:

- **Frontier Red Team**: Stress-tests AI systems to identify capability limits
- **Societal Impacts**: Studies real-world AI implementation effects
- **Economic Research**: Tracks employment and economic consequences

Jack Clark, Anthropic co-founder, leads the Institute as Head of Public Benefit.

## Founding Leadership

Three senior researchers joined the initiative:

- **Matt Botvinick** (former Google DeepMind director): Leading AI and rule of law work
- **Anton Korinek** (University of Virginia economics professor): Studying transformative AI's economic restructuring potential
- **Zoë Hitzig** (former OpenAI researcher): Connecting economics research to model development

## Public Policy Expansion

Anthropic is simultaneously expanding its Public Policy organization under Sarah Heck's leadership. The company plans to open its first Washington, D.C. office this spring and expand globally. Current policy priorities include model safety, energy protections, infrastructure investments, export controls, and AI governance.
