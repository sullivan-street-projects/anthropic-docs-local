# Ask Claude about the Anthropic Economic Index

**Date:** July 22, 2026

## Overview

Anthropic launched a new Claude connector enabling users to explore economic data directly through conversational queries. The tool provides access to the Anthropic Economic Index, which tracks AI usage patterns across the economy.

## Key Features

Users can now ask Claude questions such as:
- "Which occupations use AI the most?"
- "What are the most common ways people in Colorado use Claude?"
- "What sorts of tasks do teachers use Claude for?"
- "What kinds of tasks are people automating with AI?"

## Accessibility

The connector requires minimal setup—users can enable it through the connectors menu in claude.ai and works across all Claude models. As Anthropic notes, "The Index reflects patterns in Claude usage rather than the labor market as a whole" and will direct users to source data and its limitations.

## Purpose

The initiative aims to democratize access to economic AI research previously utilized mainly by researchers, journalists, and policymakers. Users can now explore how AI adoption varies by industry, geography, and occupation through natural conversation with Claude.
