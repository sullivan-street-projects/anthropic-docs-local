# Claude Science, an AI workbench for scientists

**Date:** June 30, 2026

---

## Introduction

Anthropic announced the launch of Claude Science, described as "an AI workbench for scientists." The platform aims to accelerate scientific discovery by integrating commonly-used research tools into a unified environment.

## Core Features

**Unified Research Environment**

Claude Science consolidates fragmented tools scientists typically use separately. Rather than toggling between PubMed, Jupyter notebooks, R, and cluster terminals, researchers can conduct their entire workflow in one space. The system produces reproducible results with complete audit trails documenting how outputs were generated.

**Advanced Capabilities**

The platform includes:
- Over 60 pre-configured skills and connectors for genomics, proteomics, and structural biology
- Multi-agent architecture with specialist agents and a reviewer agent that checks citations and calculations
- Native rendering of 3D protein structures, genome tracks, and chemical structures

**Compute Management**

Claude Science handles computational infrastructure automatically. It scales from single GPU operations to hundreds as needed, whether running on local machines, HPC clusters, or cloud resources. Sensitive datasets remain on institutional systems; only necessary context transfers to Claude.

## Real-World Applications

Early users achieved significant results:

- **Manifold Bio** leveraged the system for tissue-targeting drug candidate assessment
- **Allen Institute researcher** developed AI-powered literature review templates, reducing two-year timelines substantially
- **UCSF epidemiologist** completed germline analyses in approximately one-tenth previous timeframes

## Availability

Claude Science launched in beta for Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise subscribers on macOS and Linux. A funding program offers up to $30,000 in credits for selected projects through December 2026.
