# Advancing Claude in Healthcare and the Life Sciences

**Date:** January 11, 2026

## Overview

Anthropic announced expanded capabilities for Claude across healthcare and life sciences sectors. The announcement introduces Claude for Healthcare—a suite of HIPAA-ready tools for medical providers—alongside enhancements to Claude for Life Sciences, which now supports clinical trial operations and regulatory submissions.

## Key Developments

### Claude for Healthcare

New connectors enable healthcare organizations to access:

- **CMS Coverage Database** - Supports prior authorization checks and claims appeals
- **ICD-10 Classification System** - Facilitates medical coding and billing accuracy
- **National Provider Identifier Registry** - Enables credentialing and claims validation
- **PubMed Integration** - Provides access to 35+ million biomedical literature sources

Two new Agent Skills support FHIR development and prior authorization review workflows.

### Personal Health Data Access

Claude Pro and Max subscribers in the US can securely connect personal health records through:

- HealthEx and Function connectors (beta)
- Apple Health integration (iOS, rolling out)
- Android Health Connect (rolling out)

Users retain full control and can disconnect anytime. Health data doesn't train models.

### Expanded Life Sciences Tools

New connectors include Medidata, ClinicalTrials.gov, bioRxiv/medRxiv, Open Targets, ChEMBL, and others. Fresh Agent Skills support clinical trial protocol drafting and bioinformatics workflows.

## Model Performance

Claude Opus 4.5 demonstrates significant improvements across medical benchmarks, life sciences tasks, and spatial biology analysis. The model shows enhanced accuracy in medical calculations, clinical decision support, and reduced hallucinations.

## Customer Partnerships

Organizations including Banner Health, Novo Nordisk, Sanofi, and Elation Health are leveraging Claude for healthcare transformation initiatives.
