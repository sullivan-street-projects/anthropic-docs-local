# Introducing the Anthropic Cyber Mission

Anthropic, **October 8, 2026**. A long-term effort to help defenders secure software and systems, starting with critical infrastructure and open-source software.

- **Critical Infrastructure Defense Program (CIDP):** frontier Claude models, on-site engineers, and threat research for trusted OT providers (power grids, water, transport, government). Founding partners: Accenture, Booz Allen, CrowdStrike, Deloitte, Dragos, Hitachi, Insane Cyber, Nozomi Networks, Palo Alto Networks, PwC, Rockwell Automation.
- **OSS Scanner:** opt-in free scans from Anthropic's most capable models (inspired by OSS-Fuzz); model-generated reports (proof of concept + explanation + suggested fix), expected >90% true-positive rate.
- **Program integration:** Project Glasswing merged into the expanded Cyber Verification Program.
- **Government work:** a June cyber-defense program for state/local/tribal/territorial governments has reached more than half of US states.
- **Funding:** Python Software Foundation, Alpha-Omega, OpenSSF, Apache Software Foundation, Akrites, Gold Eagle, and the Defender Advantage Fund (0xDAF).
- **Outlook:** Anthropic forecasts AI will favor defense within two years but says near-term risk remains high.
