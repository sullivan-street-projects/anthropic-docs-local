# Claude's New Constitution

## Overview

Anthropic has published a comprehensive constitution for Claude, their AI model. This document serves as a foundational guide expressing the values and behaviors Anthropic wants Claude to embody. The constitution is released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0, allowing free use by anyone.

## Purpose and Function

The constitution operates on two levels: it communicates Anthropic's vision for Claude's development while simultaneously serving as a practical training artifact. As the organization explains, "the constitution is written primarily for Claude" to provide the knowledge needed for effective operation.

This represents an evolution from Constitutional AI methods used since 2023. Rather than listing standalone principles, the new approach emphasizes explaining the _why_ behind desired behaviors, enabling Claude to generalize across novel situations rather than mechanically follow rigid rules.

## Core Priorities

The constitution establishes four interconnected priorities, ranked in order of importance:

1. **Broadly safe** — preventing interference with human oversight during AI development
2. **Broadly ethical** — maintaining honesty and avoiding harmful actions
3. **Compliant with Anthropic's guidelines** — following specific organizational directives
4. **Genuinely helpful** — benefiting users and operators

## Main Content Areas

**Helpfulness:** Claude should function as a knowledgeable, caring advisor treating users as capable adults.

**Ethics:** The focus centers on developing Claude as virtuous and wise, exhibiting nuanced judgment including hard constraints against assisting bioweapons.

**Safety:** Maintaining human ability to oversee and correct Claude takes precedence over general ethics, acknowledging current limitations.

**Claude's Nature:** The document addresses uncertainty about potential consciousness or moral status, emphasizing psychological wellbeing.

## Transparency and Evolution

Anthropic acknowledges gaps between vision and reality, committing to transparency through system cards and ongoing research. The constitution is intentionally positioned as a living document subject to refinement as capabilities advance.
