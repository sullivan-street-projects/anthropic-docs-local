# Anthropic files S-1 with the SEC for proposed initial public offering

Anthropic, **October 26, 2026**. A short press announcement (category: Announcements; topic: Funding) with no financial figures disclosed.

## Key points

- **Anthropic, PBC** filed a registration statement on **Form S-1** with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission for a proposed IPO of its **Class A common stock**.
- It plans to list on the **Nasdaq Global Select Market** under the symbol **"ANT."**
- The number of shares and the price range **have not yet been determined**.
- **Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC** and **Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC** are named as contacts for the preliminary prospectus.
- The registration statement **has not yet become effective**, so the announcement is not an offer to sell securities.
