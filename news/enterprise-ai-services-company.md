# Building a new enterprise AI services company

**Date:** May 4, 2026

## Overview

Anthropic has partnered with Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman, and Goldman Sachs to establish a new AI services company designed to deploy Claude within mid-sized organizations across various industries. The venture also receives backing from General Atlantic, Leonard Green, Apollo Global Management, GIC, and Sequoia Capital.

## Purpose and Strategy

The new enterprise addresses a significant market gap. As Krishna Rao, Anthropic's CFO, explains: "Enterprise demand for Claude is significantly outpacing any single delivery model." The initiative targets companies—including regional health systems, community banks, and mid-sized manufacturers—that would benefit from AI implementation but lack internal capacity for frontier deployments.

Anthropic's applied AI engineers will collaborate with the firm's team to identify high-impact use cases, develop customized solutions, and provide ongoing support.

## Implementation Approach

Engagements follow a structured process beginning with close collaboration to determine where Claude creates maximum value. The company's engineers, working alongside Anthropic staff, build tailored systems integrated into existing workflows.

A healthcare example illustrates this: clinicians at physician practice networks spend considerable time on documentation, medical coding, and compliance tasks. Teams would work directly with clinical and IT staff to develop tools fitting their established processes, ultimately freeing clinicians to focus on patient care.

## Claude Partner Network Integration

The new company will join Anthropic's Claude Partner Network alongside established firms like Accenture, Deloitte, and PwC, expanding delivery capacity across enterprise sectors.
