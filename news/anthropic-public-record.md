# Results from the First Anthropic Public Record

**Date:** June 12, 2026

## Overview

Anthropic released findings from its inaugural Public Record survey, a new series designed to measure public attitudes toward AI. The survey included nearly 52,000 Americans polled in November and December 2025.

## Key Findings

### Top AI Hopes

- Disease curing (cancer, Alzheimer's): 48%
- Helping people with disabilities: 36%
- Technological progress and life improvement: 23% (tied)

### Primary Fears

- Job displacement: 64% (most common across all demographics)
- Cognitive dependency: 56%
- Misinformation: 52%

### Governance & Trust

- Over 70% support government regulation of AI (bipartisan)
- Priority areas: privacy (56%), child safety (52%), liability (49%)
- Only 15% trust AI companies to self-regulate
- Independent experts rated highest for trustworthiness at 43%

### Employment Impact

- Job loss concerns rise with education level
- Daily AI users show 54% worry versus 70% among non-users
- Most Americans oppose significant AI involvement in their jobs

### Integrated Users Profile

About 6% of Americans use AI daily for both work and personal life. These early adopters are younger, more educated, urban-based, and express fewer concerns across all harm categories.

### Governance Preferences

Americans ranked legal liability (47%) and prioritizing safety over growth (44%) as most important for ensuring beneficial AI development.
