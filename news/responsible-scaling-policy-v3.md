# Anthropic's Responsible Scaling Policy: Version 3.0

**Published:** February 24, 2026

## Overview

Anthropic unveiled the third iteration of its Responsible Scaling Policy (RSP), a voluntary framework designed to mitigate catastrophic risks from advancing AI systems. The update reflects lessons learned over two and a half years of implementation.

## Key Changes

**Three Major Elements:**

1. **Separation of Company vs. Industry Goals** — The revised policy distinguishes between Anthropic's unilateral commitments and broader recommendations for the entire AI industry.

2. **Frontier Safety Roadmap** — Ambitious yet achievable public goals spanning security, alignment, safeguards, and policy. The company commits to openly tracking progress against these targets.

3. **Risk Reports with External Review** — Comprehensive safety assessments published every 3-6 months, subject to third-party expert review in certain circumstances.

## What Worked

The original RSP successfully:
- Incentivized development of stronger internal safeguards
- Proved ASL-3 deployment standards were achievable
- Encouraged competitors like OpenAI and Google DeepMind to adopt similar frameworks
- Influenced government AI policy discussions globally

## Key Challenges

The policy encountered obstacles including:
- Ambiguous capability thresholds that hindered consensus-building
- Slower-than-expected government action on AI safety
- Higher ASL levels requiring capabilities possibly "impossible" for unilateral company implementation

The revision reflects pragmatism: acknowledging current structural limits while maintaining commitment to transparency and safety advancement.
