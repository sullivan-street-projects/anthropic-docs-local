# Introducing Claude Design by Anthropic Labs

**Date:** Apr 17, 2026

---

## Overview

Anthropic has unveiled Claude Design, a new product from Anthropic Labs that enables collaborative visual creation. The tool is powered by Claude Opus 4.7 and available to Claude Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise subscribers in research preview.

## Key Features

**Design Capabilities**

The platform supports creation of designs, prototypes, slides, one-pagers, and other visual materials. Users describe their needs and Claude generates initial versions, which can be refined through conversation, inline comments, direct edits, or custom adjustment controls.

**Brand Integration**

During setup, Claude reads team codebases and design files to build a design system. "Your colors, typography, and components" are applied automatically to subsequent projects, maintaining consistency across organizational work.

**Workflow Tools**

- Import from text prompts, images, documents (DOCX, PPTX, XLSX), or codebases
- Use web capture to grab website elements for realistic prototypes
- Adjust spacing, color, and layout with live controls
- Share designs via internal URLs or export to Canva, PDF, PPTX, and HTML

**Development Integration**

Completed designs package into handoff bundles for Claude Code implementation with minimal instruction.

## Use Cases

Teams are leveraging Claude Design for interactive prototypes, product wireframes, design explorations, pitch decks, marketing collateral, and code-powered prototypes with voice, video, and 3D elements.

## Industry Feedback

Partners including Canva, Brilliant, and Datadog have reported significant efficiency gains, with some complex prototyping tasks reduced from twenty prompts to two.

## Availability

Access is included with Claude subscriptions. Enterprise organizations can enable it through admin settings.
