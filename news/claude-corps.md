# Introducing Claude Corps

**Published:** June 11, 2026

## Overview

Anthropic launched Claude Corps, a national fellowship program placing early-career professionals in nonprofits across America. The initiative will train 1,000 fellows to use Claude AI and match them with at least 400 nonprofits for year-long, full-time positions. Anthropic is committing $150 million to establish what leadership describes as "a model for widening AI's benefits during a period of vast economic change."

## Program Structure

The partnership involves three organizations:

- **Anthropic** funds the program and provides Claude expertise
- **CodePath** serves as employer of record and leads fellow training
- **Social Finance** manages measurement and builds scalability infrastructure

## Fellow Experience

Fellows receive:

- 12-month placement
- $85,000 annual salary plus benefits
- Five hours weekly training beyond host organization work
- Access to CodePath mentors and Anthropic office hours
- Substantial Claude token budgets

Eligibility requires being over 18 with fewer than two years full-time work experience; educational background is not required.

## Host Organizations

Participating nonprofits include StriveTogether, Team Red White & Blue, Montgomery County Food Bank, REEF Environmental Education Foundation, and others spanning education, veteran services, food security, and environmental conservation.

## Timeline & Applications

- First cohort (100 fellows): Applications close July 17, 2026; begins October 2026
- Subsequent cohorts: January and August 2027
- Host organization applications open for all cohort dates

## Future Plans

Anthropic plans to open-source core program infrastructure and explore international expansion beyond the initial 1,000-fellow goal.
