# Physicist Christopher Stubbs joins Anthropic's Long-Term Benefit Trust

Anthropic, **October 21, 2026**. **Christopher Stubbs**, an experimental physicist and former Harvard Dean of Science (2018–2024), has joined Anthropic's **Long-Term Benefit Trust (LTBT)**. He becomes the Trust's first physicist.

## Who he is

Stubbs's work spans observational cosmology and precision measurement, and he has advised the U.S. government on national security and arms control. In 1998 he helped discover that the universe's expansion is accelerating — a finding later recognized with the Breakthrough Prize in Fundamental Physics. He was the inaugural project scientist for what is now the **Vera C. Rubin Observatory**, and is a member of **JASON**, an independent group of scientists that advises the U.S. government.

## Why it matters for governance

The Trust can **appoint a majority of Anthropic's board** and advises the board and leadership on major decisions, especially those involving AI's risks and societal impacts. Its trustees are independent of management and investors. Because Anthropic is a **Public Benefit Corporation** that must balance commercial growth with its public mission, the Trust is designed to help it keep that balance over time. The article presents Stubbs's appointment as strengthening scientific rigor in oversight of AI development.
