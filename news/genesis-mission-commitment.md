# Building on our commitment to American scientific discovery

Anthropic, **October 8, 2026**. A **$150 million, three-year** commitment to the Genesis Mission, a federal initiative to accelerate scientific and technological discovery through AI. Funding makes Claude available to 15+ participating agencies, including NASA, NIH, and NSF. Announced at the "Science: A New Golden Age" Summit hosted by the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy.

- **Background:** builds on Anthropic's December DOE partnership and work bringing Claude to national-laboratory scientists.
- **Support:** Claude, Claude Code, and API credits to several hundred Genesis Mission research projects over three years.
- **Priority areas:** fusion energy and quantum computing.
- **Training:** onboarding and technical support for scientists; help for agencies new to the mission.
- **Related:** Claude Science workbench, 10,000 free/discounted academic seats, expanded AI for Science credits, and a research preview of the Model Hardware Standard for AI agents operating lab instruments.
