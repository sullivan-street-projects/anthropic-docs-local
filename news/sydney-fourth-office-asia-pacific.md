# Sydney will become Anthropic's fourth office in Asia-Pacific

**Date:** March 10, 2026

Anthropic is establishing its fourth Asia-Pacific office in Sydney, joining existing locations in Tokyo, Bengaluru, and Seoul. This expansion addresses growing demand from businesses in Australia and New Zealand seeking AI solutions tailored to their regional needs.

## Expansion Details

### Regional Engagement
The company plans to deepen partnerships with Australian institutions and collaborate on initiatives supporting national priorities. An executive team visit to Australia at the end of March will formalize these relationships and meet with customers and policymakers.

### Current Market Presence
Australia and New Zealand rank 4th and 8th globally in Claude.ai usage relative to population. Both nations demonstrate strong adoption for coding tasks, educational applications, and research activities.

## Focus Areas

**Initial priorities include:**
- Supporting enterprise, startup, and research clients
- Partnerships with organizations like Canva, Quantium, and Commonwealth Bank of Australia
- Emerging sectors including AgTech, physical AI, and climate technology

## Infrastructure Plans

Anthropic is exploring compute capacity expansion in Australia through third-party partners using existing infrastructure. This addresses data residency requirements from enterprises and government agencies. The company is also in early discussions about longer-term regional infrastructure development.
