# A Research Agenda for the Economic Futures Research Fund

**Date:** July 22, 2026

## Overview

Anthropic announced a $200 million Economic Futures Research Fund designed to support external research on how society can prepare for AI's economic impacts. The initiative aims to build empirical evidence about interventions that could make economies more flexible and resilient while ensuring AI's benefits are broadly shared.

## Five Research Priorities

### 1. Workplace-Level AI Integration

Research examining how firms design AI systems and collaborative patterns. Focus areas include field experiments testing different organizational approaches and their effects on productivity and worker outcomes.

### 2. Equipping Workers for Transitions

Studies on retraining, job placement, and sectoral transition programs. The fund seeks evidence on innovative models including AI-enabled matching services and bundled support combining income assistance with reemployment services.

### 3. Modernizing Income Support

Research into unemployment insurance reforms suited to persistent displacement scenarios, including alternative eligibility structures, basic needs relief, and unconditional income pilots examining labor supply, wellbeing, and civic participation effects.

### 4. Building Worker Ownership in AI Growth

Testing mechanisms like universal pre-distributive capital accounts and equity-sharing arrangements. Research will evaluate different revenue-generation approaches and their economic impacts.

### 5. Public Investment Evidence

Large-scale pilots funding human-facing services and measuring employment, educational, and wellbeing outcomes. Includes guaranteed-jobs programs and place-based community interventions.

## Funding Parameters

- **Grant Range:** Primarily $5-30 million
- **Minimum:** $1 million
- **Eligible Applicants:** Accredited universities, independent research institutes, nonprofits with field experiment experience
- **Geographic Focus:** Primarily US-based, though global applications welcome

The fund emphasizes ambitious, creative proposals over incremental research and prioritizes early sharing of findings to enable timely policy adaptation.
