# Anthropic Invests $100 Million into the Claude Partner Network

**Date:** March 12, 2026

## Overview

Anthropic has launched the Claude Partner Network, committing $100 million to support partner organizations helping enterprises adopt Claude. Partners gain access to training, technical support, and joint market development resources.

## Key Announcements

### Claude Partner Network Benefits

Partners receive:

- Access to Anthropic Academy training materials
- Sales playbooks from Anthropic's go-to-market team
- Co-marketing documentation
- Listing in the Services Partner Directory for enterprise buyers
- Dedicated Applied AI engineers for customer implementations
- Technical architects for complex deployments
- Localized go-to-market support internationally

### New Claude Technical Certification

"Claude Certified Architect, Foundations" launches today—a technical exam for solution architects building production applications. Additional certifications for sellers, architects, and developers will roll out later in 2026.

### Code Modernization Starter Kit

A new resource helps partners assist enterprises in migrating legacy codebases and addressing technical debt.

## Membership

Membership is free and open to any organization bringing Claude to market. Applications opened March 12, 2026.

## Partner Testimonials

**Accenture** (Alex Holt): Training 30,000 professionals on Claude; the network accelerates practice-building and team deployment.

**Deloitte** (Ranjit Bawa): The network formalizes enterprise AI deployment work through training and practical guidance.

**Cognizant** (Sandra Notardonato): Supporting 350,000 associates; the network enables faster progression from pilots to production.

**Infosys** (Anand Swaminathan): Dedicated Center of Excellence helps clients scale AI with robust governance and security.

## Claude Availability

Claude remains the only frontier AI model available across all three major cloud providers: AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure.
