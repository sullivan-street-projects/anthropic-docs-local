# Introducing the Life Sciences Verification Program

**Publication Date:** September 17, 2026

Anthropic has launched the Life Sciences Verification Program (LSVP), providing life science professionals with access to Mythos, Opus, and Sonnet models featuring refined safeguards tailored for biology-related research. The program aims to enable work currently restricted in standard models, including drug discovery, research biology, clinical development, and manufacturing.

## Verification and Access Types

Organizations undergo verification examining research credentials, security standards, and ethical oversight. Two grant types are available:

- **Standard Use** grants serve most life science work across basic science, R&D, supply chain, manufacturing, clinical development, quality assurance, and regulatory affairs. These grants extend to entire teams and renew annually, providing access to Mythos 5.1, Opus 5, and Sonnet 5.
- **High-risk Use** grants remove all life sciences safeguards for specific projects requiring additional vetting. These apply to single research projects rather than full teams and require six-month renewals. High-risk grants for Opus 5 and Sonnet 5 are currently available; Mythos access remains limited pending U.S. government coordination.

Other safeguards, including cyber classifiers, remain active under LSVP grants.

## Enabling Trusted Access Through Shared Responsibility

The program addresses three threat models: access compromise through malware or account takeover, insider threats from rogue employees, and agent misuse during extended operations.

Safeguards operate on shared responsibility principles, monitoring usage against specified intended use-cases. Organizations define safe usage parameters, and Anthropic continuously monitors traffic for unauthorized patterns, flagging concerning activity to organization administrators.

## How Monitoring Works in LSVP

The program shifts enforcement from real-time request blocking to offline monitoring, enabling identification of misuse across behavioral patterns. This approach allows legitimate work to proceed with fewer interruptions while requiring 30-day data retention for flagged activity. Such data remains compartmentalized, unavailable for model training or access by Anthropic's life sciences research teams.

## Applications and Availability

Interested organizations may apply at the LSVP application portal. The program expects to enroll hundreds of organizations initially, expanding to support the broader life sciences community.

LSVP is currently available through Claude's first-party console for API usage and in Claude for Enterprise and Team plans, but not yet for individual plans or third-party platforms. BAA-enabled organizations cannot currently participate, as the program is in beta.

## What Comes Next

Anthropic plans to share announcements regarding new products, research collaborations, and program improvements in coming months as part of broader efforts supporting the life sciences community.
