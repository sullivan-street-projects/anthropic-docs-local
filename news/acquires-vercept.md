# Anthropic Acquires Vercept to Advance Claude's Computer Use Capabilities

**Published:** February 25, 2026

Anthropic has announced the acquisition of Vercept, a company focused on advancing AI perception and interaction capabilities. The acquisition aims to enhance Claude's computer use abilities.

## Key Points

**Background on Vercept:**
Vercept was built on the principle that "making AI genuinely useful for completing complex tasks requires solving hard perception and interaction problems." The company's founding team includes Kiana Ehsani, Luca Weihs, and Ross Girshick. Vercept will discontinue its external product and integrate into Anthropic's operations.

**Claude's Progress:**
The timing follows Claude Sonnet 4.6's release, which demonstrates significant improvements in computer use capabilities. On the OSWorld evaluation benchmark, Claude's performance improved from under 15% in late 2024 to 72.5% currently, approaching human-level performance on tasks like spreadsheet navigation and web form completion.

**Strategic Rationale:**
Anthropic seeks teams whose technical ambitions align with theirs and whose work advances capabilities while maintaining safety and rigor principles. This acquisition follows Anthropic's previous acquisition of Bun.

**Hiring:**
Anthropic is actively recruiting for its engineering team, with interested candidates directed to the careers page.
