# 2026 Usage Policy update

Anthropic, published **October 8, 2026**, effective **November 12, 2026**. Most changes clarify existing rules and add examples for Claude's longer, more independent work, drawing on misuse patterns from the September 2026 threat intelligence report.

**Key changes:**
- **New deceptive-activity section** ("Do Not Engage in Deceptive Campaigns or Artificial Activity") consolidates rules on fake accounts, fabricated news sites, and influence operations, including building the tools for such campaigns.
- **Elections section renamed** to "Do Not Undermine Democratic Processes." The blanket ban on personalized vote/campaign targeting was removed, though deceptive or personal-data-based targeting stays prohibited elsewhere.
- **Weapons:** now explicitly covers guidance/control software, weapon components, and arming drones/autonomous vehicles.
- **Surveillance & law enforcement:** rewritten to bar non-consensual tracking (real-time or via collected data); Claude cannot decide/recommend who to investigate, arrest, or charge, nor be used to build surveillance tools. Consented tracking, content moderation, journalism, and legal research remain permitted.
- **High-risk use cases:** human-in-the-loop and notice requirements unchanged; new requirements when Claude controls hardware taking autonomous physical actions (a qualified operator must be able to observe and stop it; it must hold a safe state if Claude disconnects).
- **Abusive behavior toward models:** new prohibition on sustained, needless abuse/cruelty toward models (extreme cases only).
- **Supported Regions:** clarified enforcement by physical location, incorporation/HQ, and majority ownership/control.
