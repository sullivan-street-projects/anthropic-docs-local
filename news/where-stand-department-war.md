# Where Things Stand with the Department of War

**Date:** March 5, 2026
**Author:** Dario Amodei

## Opening Statement

Anthropic received a letter from the Department of War on March 4, 2026, designating the company as a supply chain risk to national security. The organization disputes this action's legal foundation and intends to challenge it in court.

## Scope of the Designation

The Department's letter applies narrowly to Claude usage that directly supports Department of War contracts. The vast majority of Anthropic's customers remain unaffected by this designation. The restriction does not extend to broader business relationships between contractors and Anthropic when those uses are unrelated to specific military contracts.

## Legal Framework

The relevant statute, 10 USC 3252, exists to protect government interests rather than punish suppliers. The law mandates use of the "least restrictive means necessary" to accomplish supply chain protection goals.

## Prior Engagement

Anthropic had been conducting productive discussions with the Department of War regarding potential service arrangements complying with the company's two narrow exceptions, as well as transition planning if collaboration becomes impossible.

## Company Exceptions

Anthropic maintains two specific policy limitations: fully autonomous weapons systems and mass domestic surveillance. These relate to high-level usage areas, not operational decision-making.

## Internal Post Apology

Amodei apologized for a leaked internal company post written six days prior, characterizing it as emotionally reactive and no longer representative of careful consideration.

## Current Commitment

Anthropic pledges to provide models to the Department of War and national security community at minimal cost with ongoing engineering support during necessary transitions, provided legally permitted.

## Alignment Statement

Both Anthropic and the Department of War share commitment to advancing U.S. national security and defending Americans while prioritizing AI application across government.
