# Preparing for the 2026 US midterms

Anthropic, **October 14, 2026**. How Anthropic is strengthening election safeguards for the US midterms, building on earlier work on political bias, misuse detection, and directing people to accurate voting information.

## How people use Claude for politics

A random sample of 250,000 US consumer conversations (Sept 21–27, 2026): ~5.75% touched politics/policy/social issues; ~0.51% concerned elections or voting; ~0.01% asked whom/what to vote for; ~0.002% violated election-integrity policies. Most users wanted information to reach their own conclusions. Political/election queries are expected to rise toward Election Day.

## Connecting users to trusted information

Claude shows a banner linking to **TurboVote** (Democracy Works) for registration/polling/date questions. Later in October, Claude will pull official voting information from Democracy Works for US users — state-specific answers on where to vote and what ID to bring, within the conversation. On election night it will show current official vote counts and results.

## Measuring and reducing political bias

Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, and Haiku 5.5 scored **99%** on Anthropic's political even-handedness evaluation. Three independent groups reviewed free-expression handling (Future of Free Speech at Vanderbilt; Foundation for American Innovation; Collective Intelligence Project, surveying 2,000+ across US/UK/India). Weaknesses found: unrequested caveats/counterarguments; Sonnet 4.5 hedged unevenly (softening right-leaning arguments). Anthropic has improved yes/no and short-answer responses and is refining when caveats appear.

## Defining and preventing misuse

The Usage Policy prohibits deceiving voters or disrupting elections (false info, impersonating officials, suppressing turnout); a 2026 policy update clarifies election rules. A campaign guide lists permitted uses (drafting materials, debate prep, turnout analysis) and prohibited ones (fake personas, mass-produced engagement, posing as independent news).

## Testing and strengthening defenses

Automated classifiers detect election abuse. Testing with the Institute for Strategic Dialogue and McCain Institute (US, Israel, Brazil) found Claude reliably refused individual harmful requests but could miss campaigns split into innocuous tasks. Anthropic added classifiers for multi-step misuse, fake accounts/news sites, financially motivated schemes, and foreign state actors, and strengthened cyber protections for election infrastructure.

## Observed threats

The September 2026 threat-intelligence report found political, state-aligned, and commercial actors using AI across influence operations — including foreign operators producing midterm coverage for a fake news site favoring Russian, Iranian, and North Korean narratives. Involved accounts were banned.
