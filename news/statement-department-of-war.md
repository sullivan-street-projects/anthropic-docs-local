# Statement from Dario Amodei on our discussions with the Department of War

**Published:** February 26, 2026

## Overview

Dario Amodei, CEO of Anthropic, issued a statement addressing negotiations with the Department of War regarding AI deployment in military and national security contexts. The statement outlines Anthropic's commitment to national defense while explaining two specific safeguards the company maintains.

## Anthropic's National Security Engagement

Amodei emphasized that Anthropic has proactively deployed Claude across military and intelligence operations, claiming to be "the first frontier AI company" to deploy models in classified U.S. government networks and at National Laboratories. Claude is extensively used for intelligence analysis, operational planning, and cyber operations.

## Two Non-Negotiable Safeguards

Anthropic refuses to provide AI for two applications:

1. **Mass Domestic Surveillance** — The company opposes using AI systems for large-scale domestic surveillance, which it contends "undermines democratic values." Amodei argues that "powerful AI makes it possible to assemble scattered data into a comprehensive picture of any person's life—automatically and at massive scale."

2. **Fully Autonomous Weapons** — While supporting partially autonomous systems used in Ukraine, Anthropic maintains that "frontier AI systems are simply not reliable enough" for fully autonomous weapons without human oversight.

## Government Pressure

The Department of War demanded Anthropic remove these safeguards, threatening to revoke their contracts and designate the company a "supply chain risk"—a designation normally reserved for U.S. adversaries.

## Anthropic's Position

Despite threats, Amodei stated the company "cannot in good conscience" comply. However, Anthropic expressed willingness to facilitate a smooth transition if the government chooses another provider.
