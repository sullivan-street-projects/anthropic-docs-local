## Landmark Papers

These foundational papers define Anthropic's research directions:

### Constitutional AI (Dec 2022)

Introduced harmlessness from AI feedback, establishing the constitutional approach to alignment that underpins Claude's training.

### Sleeper Agents (Jan 2024)

Demonstrated that deceptive behaviors can persist through safety training, highlighting challenges in AI safety.

### Transformer Circuits Framework (Dec 2021)

Established the mechanistic interpretability approach for understanding neural network internals.

### Toy Models of Superposition (Sep 2022)

Revealed how neural networks pack more concepts than dimensions, explaining polysemanticity.

### RLHF Training (Apr 2022)

Foundational work on training helpful and harmless assistants using reinforcement learning from human feedback.
