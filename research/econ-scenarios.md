# Scenarios for our Economic Future

Anthropic Institute, **September 2026** (v1.0 of the Econ Scenario Explorer). Anthropic's Economics team built a model of how AI might affect US jobs, growth, and unemployment, accompanied by an interactive explorer that lets readers enter their own predictions about AI capabilities and adoption and see the 2030 economy those predictions imply.

## Method

The model treats the economy as bundles of tasks. Tasks can be left unchanged, augmented by AI, automated, or newly created by AI.

## Three Scenarios

- **Modest:** AI's effect is similar to the internet's, with gains within historical norms.
- **Substantial:** AI can do about half of knowledge work by 2030, and the economy grows at twice its normal rate.
- **Extreme:** AI outperforms humans at most knowledge tasks, growth reaches 15% a year, and unemployment could rise beyond typical recession levels.

## Public Survey

More than 10,000 Americans were surveyed (fielded August 2026). The typical answers implied something close to the substantial scenario: GDP about 10% higher than without AI by 2030, and unemployment near 5%.

## Key Findings

- AI raises GDP in all scenarios. The 2030 projections are approximately $34.1T (modest), $36.3T (substantial), and $44.4T (extreme).
- Knowledge workers face more job switching. Unemployment rises for them while it falls in other occupations.
- Average wages rise, but gains concentrate outside knowledge work. Knowledge-worker wages are roughly flat in the substantial scenario and fall by more than 10% in the extreme one.
- Labor's share of output shrinks as capital's share grows, so the main challenge becomes distributing gains broadly.

## Caveats

The model is Version 1.0 and omits policy responses, business cycles, data center demand effects, and hyper-capable robots. The article states, "The future is not predetermined."
