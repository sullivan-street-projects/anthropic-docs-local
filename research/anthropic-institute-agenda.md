# Focus Areas for The Anthropic Institute

**Published:** May 7, 2026

## Overview

The Anthropic Institute (TAI) announced its research agenda focusing on four key areas investigating AI's societal impact. The institute leverages Anthropic's position as a frontier lab to conduct research and share findings publicly.

## Four Research Pillars

### 1. Economic Diffusion

Examines how powerful AI systems reshape economies, addressing questions about global AI adoption, firm-level implementation, productivity growth, labor market transformation, and wealth distribution mechanisms. The institute plans to expand its Economic Index data offerings and monthly surveys tracking workforce perceptions.

### 2. Threats and Resilience

Investigates dual-use AI capabilities that could enable security threats while developing societal resilience measures. Research examines cyber and biological risks, offensive-defense balancing, geopolitical crisis infrastructure needs, and surveillance implications.

### 3. AI Systems in the Wild

Studies how people and institutions interact with deployed AI systems, focusing on epistemology shifts, critical thinking degradation, human-AI team management, behavioral effects, and governing autonomous agents through existing legal frameworks.

### 4. AI-Driven R&D

Analyzes how AI systems autonomously conduct research, including potential "recursive self-improvement" scenarios. The institute explores governance mechanisms, early warning systems for acceleration, and how AI development unevenly impacts different scientific fields.

## Key Initiatives

- **Anthropic Economic Index:** Enhanced data sharing on labor impacts and AI usage patterns
- **Frontier Red Team:** Developing threat assessment capabilities
- **Anthropic Fellowship:** Four-month funded opportunities for external researchers
- **Long-Term Benefit Trust Integration:** Research informing company decisions
