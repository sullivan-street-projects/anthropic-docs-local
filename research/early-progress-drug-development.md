# Early progress on accelerating drug development with Claude

Anthropic, **October 27, 2026**. Anthropic's life sciences team reports early results from deep research partnerships with six drug developers — **BigHat Biosciences, Bristol Myers Squibb, Divergence Labs, Enveda, Generate Biomedicines, and The Column Group** — plus Anthropic's own discovery programs. The thesis: Claude is useful across many stages of drug development, not only molecule design.

## Partner case studies

- **Generate Biomedicines (CAR T-cell therapy):** Claude Code proposed shorter genetic designs — one roughly **70% shorter** — that let immune cells make about **10x** as many receptors and kill tumor cells more efficiently.
- **Divergence Labs (food allergies):** Claude generated about **35,000 antibody designs** in ~1 month; three bound IgE, and 3 of 20 dislodging candidates beat the standard medication in early tests.
- **BigHat Biosciences (lead optimization):** ~**1,010 variants** tested over ~10 weeks; after Claude reanalyzed the data, the best bound **>10x** more tightly and one stayed soluble **18°C** higher.
- **Enveda (manufacturing/filings):** On **1,061 pages**, Claude caught all 11 human-found issues plus 7 more; review time fell **89%** (25+ hours to under 3). Drafted a regulatory document in 4 hours vs. ~3 months by hand.
- **The Column Group / Pleiades:** First Claude skill (target assessment) evaluated on **45 target-disease pairs**.
- **Bristol Myers Squibb:** Claude assesses early trial plans and success likelihood; expansion planned for 2027.
- **Mithril Medicine (acquired by Anthropic):** A muscular dystrophy candidate went from idea to promising mouse results in **under 18 months**; human trials possible as soon as late 2028.

## Caveats and next steps

Work is **still early and mostly preclinical**. Anthropic plans to extend Claude into clinical trials and more organizations, and points to **Claude Science** (an AI workbench), credits for life scientists, a Life Sciences Verification Program, and an open call for guest investigators.
