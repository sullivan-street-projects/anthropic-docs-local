# Anthropic Economic Index Report: Learning Curves

**Date:** March 24, 2026

## Overview

Anthropic's Economic Index tracks Claude usage patterns across the economy using privacy-preserving data analysis. This report analyzes Claude usage from February 5-12, 2026, examining how users adopt and learn to work with AI systems.

## Key Findings

### Usage Diversification

The concentration of Claude's top 10 tasks declined from 24% in November 2025 to 19% in February 2026 on Claude.ai. However, coding tasks have migrated toward API-based workflows rather than disappearing entirely. Personal use increased from 35% to 42% of conversations, while coursework dropped from 19% to 12%.

### Model Selection and Task Complexity

Users strategically select the more powerful Opus model for higher-value work. "For every additional $10 of hourly wage for a task, the share of conversations using Opus increases by 1.5 percentage points for Claude.ai users." API users show even stronger task-awareness, with twice the responsiveness to task complexity.

### Learning Curves and User Experience

Experienced users demonstrate measurably better outcomes. Users active for 6+ months show 10% higher conversation success rates compared to newer users, even after controlling for task type. These veteran users also employ Claude more collaboratively and tackle more sophisticated work requiring higher education levels.

### Geographic Patterns

U.S. adoption continues converging across states, though at a slower pace than previously estimated. Global adoption, conversely, has become slightly more concentrated among high-income countries.

## Implications

The research suggests potential skill-biased technological change, where early adopters with technical expertise gain compounding advantages through learning-by-doing while experiencing greater AI-assisted productivity gains.
