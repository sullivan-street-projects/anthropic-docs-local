# What do you want from AI?

Anthropic (Societal Impacts), **September 29, 2026**. A study using **Anthropic Interviewer**, an AI conducting ~15-minute interviews about people's experiences with AI (study ran Sept 29–Oct 6, 2026; open to Free/Pro/Max users of Claude and Claude Code with accounts ≥2 weeks old).

Three questions: most meaningful AI experiences (positive/negative); what about how the world works you'd like AI to help change; what you want from the companies developing AI.

**Takeaways:**
- Builds on a December 2025 study of 81,000 people; updated questions, more products, and (for the first time) optional public publication.
- Publishing is opt-in and includes country but excludes account details; the decision is effectively permanent (Anthropic can remove its copy but not others'). Re-identification risk from details like age/job/city is flagged.
- Participants can review the transcript before deciding and end early (permanently deleting the partial record). Non-public interviews are still analyzed; aggregated/de-identified findings may be published. Not a representative sample.
