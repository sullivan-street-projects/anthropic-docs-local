# Societal Impacts Research

Anthropic's societal impacts research examines how AI systems affect work, economics, and society. This includes productivity studies, bias analysis, and understanding real-world AI usage patterns.

## Latest Work (2026)

### Anthropic Economic Index Report: Cadences (Jun 26, 2026)

Examines when users engage with Claude, what they create, and their perceptions of AI's workplace impact. Part of the ongoing Economic Index research series.

### Agentic Coding and Persistent Returns to Expertise (Jun 16, 2026)

Economic research examining how expertise continues to matter even as AI coding agents become more capable.

### Coding Agents in the Social Sciences (May 27, 2026)

Research on how AI coding agents are being applied in social science research contexts.

### What 81,000 People Want from AI (Mar 18, 2026)

Nearly 81,000 people participated in the largest and most multilingual qualitative study of its kind regarding AI usage, concerns, and desires.

### Anthropic Education Report: The AI Fluency Index (Feb 23, 2026)

Measures whether individuals are developing AI fluency -- the skills needed for safe, effective collaboration with AI tools. Uses the 4D AI Fluency Framework with 24 behaviors. Key finding: iterative conversations show roughly double the fluency behaviors compared to single-response interactions. Also identified the "Artifact Paradox" where polished AI outputs reduce critical evaluation.

### Measuring AI Agent Autonomy in Practice (Feb 18, 2026)

Analysis of millions of human-agent interactions across Claude Code and the public API. Key findings: Claude Code autonomous session length nearly doubled in 3 months (from ~25 min to ~45 min). Experienced users auto-approve more but also interrupt more often, suggesting a shift from reviewing every action to intervening only when needed.

### India Country Brief: Anthropic Economic Index (Feb 16, 2026)

India is the world's second-largest market for Claude.ai usage (5.8% of global conversations). Tasks requiring 3.8 hours complete in ~15 minutes (15x speedup vs. 12x globally). Software development comprises 45.2% of tasks, highest proportion internationally.

### Anthropic Economic Index: Building Blocks for Understanding AI Use (Jan 15, 2026)

New analytical framework for measuring and understanding AI usage across the economy.

## Work from 2025

### Anthropic Interviewer (Dec 4, 2025)

Insights from interviews with 1,250 professionals across industries about their experiences working with AI tools. Key findings on adoption patterns, productivity perceptions, and workflow integration.

### How AI is Transforming Work at Anthropic (Dec 2, 2025)

Internal study of Claude Code usage among Anthropic engineers, demonstrating that "AI use is radically changing the nature of work." Provides first-party data on AI-augmented software development.

### Estimating AI Productivity Gains (Nov 25, 2025)

**Major finding:** Analysis of 100,000 conversations shows AI reduces task completion time by 80% on average.

Methodology:

- Analyzed real usage data across task types
- Controlled for task complexity
- Measured time-to-completion vs. baseline estimates

Implications for economic modeling of AI impact.

### Anthropic Economic Index: Uneven Geographic and Enterprise AI Adoption (Nov 2025)

Published on arXiv ([2511.15080](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15080)). Documents patterns of Claude usage over time, in 150+ countries, across US states, and among businesses deploying Claude through the API. Found that users increasingly entrust Claude with more autonomy, with directive task delegation rising from 27% to 39% in eight months.

## Privacy-Preserving Research

- Enables understanding of how people actually use AI

- Common use case taxonomies
- Failure mode identification
- Usage pattern evolution over time

| Metric                 | Finding                                |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Average time reduction | 80%                                    |
| India-specific speedup | 15x (vs. 12x global)                   |

| Area                    | Status                          |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------- |

### Usage Patterns (from Clio and Economic Index)

- Coding assistance: ~40-50% of interactions
- Writing help: ~25%
- Information seeking: ~20%
- Creative tasks: ~15%
- Work-related usage: 46% globally, 51.3% in India

## Implications for Practice

### For Organizations

1. **Productivity gains are real** - 80% time reduction is substantial
2. **Adoption requires change management** - Workflows must adapt
3. **Bias monitoring needed** - Regular audits recommended
4. **AI fluency matters** - Iterative engagement doubles effectiveness

### For Developers

1. **Build with privacy in mind** - Clio shows aggregate analysis is possible
2. **Test for bias systematically** - Red teaming methods available
3. **Consider global users** - Opinion representation matters
4. **Design for learning** - AI tools should support skill development, not just productivity

### For Policymakers

1. **Economic disruption is significant** - 80% productivity gains reshape labor
2. **Bias is measurable and addressable** - Technical solutions exist
3. **Privacy-preserving research is possible** - Clio demonstrates methods
4. **Global participation matters** - 81,000 person study shows diverse perspectives

## Research Methodology Notes

Anthropic's societal research emphasizes:

- **Real-world data** over synthetic benchmarks
- **Privacy preservation** in all analysis
- **Quantitative rigor** with statistical controls
- **Transparency** about limitations and uncertainties
- **Large-scale participation** (81K interviews, millions of interactions)
